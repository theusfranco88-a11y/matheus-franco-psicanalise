# Design Direction — Matheus Franco

## Positioning

A premium, restrained and cinematic digital presence for clinical psychoanalysis. The experience should feel authored, quiet and psychologically resonant — never like a generic health landing page.

## Narrative arc

1. **Dor** — something keeps repeating.
2. **Reconhecimento** — repetition is perceived rather than explained away.
3. **Reflexão** — the visitor slows down and sees themselves in the narrative.
4. **Trajetória** — professional authority appears through lived context, study and clinical formation.
5. **Escuta** — clarify what psychoanalytic work is and is not.
6. **Decisão** — invite contact without aggressive sales language.

## Visual language

- Editorial rather than corporate.
- Black, warm off-white and restrained bronze accents.
- Large serif typography paired with neutral sans-serif body copy.
- Strong negative space.
- Fine rules, subtle grain and low-contrast layers.
- Photography should feel documentary/cinematic, not stock-like.
- Real institutional imagery should be used as proof of trajectory, not decorative logo walls.

## Motion principles

- Motion supports meaning; it does not decorate empty space.
- Prefer slow reveal, parallax depth, masking and text sequencing.
- Avoid excessive bouncing, spinning or dashboard-style microinteractions.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Mobile must remain smooth and readable before adding any heavy effect.

## Content principles

- Lead with latent pain and recognition, not credentials.
- Avoid diagnostic promises, cure claims or manipulative urgency.
- Use concrete human language.
- Professional authority should emerge progressively.

## Core phrase

> O que não é dito… retorna.
